# Glossary

The ~150 named entities in this curriculum whose meaning you will need to look up at some point. Module references point to the full treatment.

## A–C

**ADC** (Asymmetric Distance Computation) — PQ distance via a per-query $m\times256$ lookup table; the query stays un-quantised. (3.7)
**Aleatoric uncertainty** — irreducible noise in the data. More data does not help. (S.12)
**Aliasing** — high frequencies masquerading as low ones after downsampling without a low-pass prefilter. (1.4, 2.1)
**Anchor** — a pre-defined box prior at each feature-map location. (4.3)
**Anchor-free** — detection without box priors; predicts distances to box sides (FCOS) or centre heatmaps (CenterNet). (4.5)
**Aperture problem** — only the flow component along the gradient is observable; equivalently, the structure tensor is rank-1 on an edge. (5.7)
**ArcFace** — additive **angular** margin softmax; replaced triplet loss for face recognition. (3.5)
**Arithmetic intensity** — FLOPs per byte of memory traffic; determines whether an op is compute- or bandwidth-bound. (2.7)
**ATSS** — adaptive label assignment; showed anchor-based ≈ anchor-free once assignment is controlled. (4.5)
**AUPRO / AUROC** — anomaly-detection metrics, per-region and per-pixel/image. (A4.2)
**Bag of Visual Words (BoVW)** — tf-idf + inverted index over quantised local descriptors. (3.2)
**Batch-hard mining** — take the hardest positive and negative *within the mini-batch*. (3.5)
**BEV** (bird's-eye view) — top-down grid representation; objects don't overlap and scale is range-invariant. (A5.1)
**BlurPool** — anti-aliased downsampling; restores shift-consistency to CNNs. (2.1)
**Boundary IoU** — IoU restricted to a band around the boundary; catches what mIoU hides. (4.12)
**Cheirality** — the constraint that reconstructed points lie in front of both cameras; disambiguates the 4-fold $E$ decomposition. (5.5)
**CLAHE** — contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalisation; the clip limit prevents noise amplification. (A1.1)
**Codebook collapse** — most VQ codebook entries go unused. (6.15b)
**Conformal prediction** — outputs a prediction *set* with a distribution-free coverage guarantee. (S.12)
**Connected components** — labelling maximally-connected foreground regions; 4- vs 8-connectivity changes the count. (A1.2)
**Coreset** — a subsampled set preserving coverage; makes PatchCore's memory bank tractable. (A4.2)
**Cross-ratio** — the projective invariant of four collinear points; enables single-view metrology. (5.4)

## D–G

**DDIM** — non-Markovian, deterministic diffusion sampling; enables few steps *and* editing. (6.13)
**DEGENSAC** — RANSAC variant that detects plane-induced degeneracy in $F$ estimation. (1.8, 5.4)
**Dense CRF** — the FCN-era boundary-refinement post-process; obsolete once decoders improved. (A1.4)
**DiT** — Diffusion Transformer; replaces the U-Net backbone. (6.13)
**DLT** — Direct Linear Transform; the linear homography/fundamental-matrix solver. (1.9)
**Effective receptive field (ERF)** — the Gaussian-weighted region that actually influences a unit; grows as $O(\sqrt L)$. (2.3)
**Epipole** — the image of one camera's centre in the other camera; the null vector of $F$. (5.5)
**Epistemic uncertainty** — uncertainty about the model, reducible with more data. (S.12)
**FGSM / PGD** — one-step and iterative gradient-based adversarial attacks. (S.11)
**FID** — Fréchet Inception Distance; distribution-level image quality *and* diversity; blind to prompt adherence. (6.12)
**FSQ** — finite scalar quantisation; a codebook-free alternative to VQ. (6.15b)
**Gauge freedom** — the 7-DoF similarity invariance of bundle adjustment; makes $\mathcal{H}$ singular. (5.8)
**GeM pooling** — generalised-mean pooling with learnable $p$; beats GAP for retrieval. (3.2)
**Gradient masking** — a defence that hides gradients without conferring robustness; fools its own evaluation. (S.11)
**Gram anchoring** — DINOv3's technique for preserving dense-feature quality over long training. (6.6)
**GrabCut** — interactive segmentation alternating GMM colour models with graph min-cut. (A1.4)

## H–M

**Hartley normalisation** — centroid to origin, RMS distance $\sqrt2$; mandatory before DLT. (1.9)
**HNSW** — hierarchical navigable small-world graph; a skip-list in metric space. (3.7)
**HOTA** — tracking metric factorising into detection × association accuracy. (5.7)
**Hough transform** — parameter-space voting; $\rho = x\cos\theta + y\sin\theta$. (A1.3)
**Hungarian algorithm** — optimal bipartite matching in $O(N^3)$; used by DETR and SORT. (4.8, 5.7)
**iBOT** — masked-image-modelling objective combined with DINO in DINOv2. (6.6)
**ICP** — Iterative Closest Point; point-to-plane converges much faster than point-to-point. (A5.3)
**InfoNCE** — the contrastive loss; a $(2N{-}1)$-way softmax classification. (3.6)
**IVF** — inverted-file index; k-means cells with an `nprobe` recall dial. (3.7)
**Label assignment** — deciding which predictions are positive for which ground truth; the modern detector battleground. (4.5)
**Lie algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$** — the axis–angle parameterisation used for rotation in optimisation. (5.2)
**Linear probe** — freeze the backbone, train a linear classifier; the standard SSL evaluation. (3.6)
**LPIPS** — learned perceptual similarity; used in VQGAN and the SD autoencoder. (6.14, 6.15b)
**MAGSAC++** — threshold-free RANSAC via $\sigma$-consensus. (1.8)
**MaskGIT** — parallel masked token decoding; ~10 steps instead of 1024. (6.15b)
**Mask2Former** — unifies semantic/instance/panoptic as mask classification. (4.10)
**Matryoshka embeddings** — embeddings whose prefixes are themselves valid embeddings. (3.1)
**mCE** — mean corruption error on ImageNet-C. (S.11)
**MIPS** — maximum inner-product search; not a metric, breaks index pruning guarantees. (3.3)
**Morphological opening / closing** — erode-then-dilate (removes small objects at original size) / dilate-then-erode (fills holes). (A1.2)

## N–R

**NMS** — greedy suppression of overlapping detections; fails in crowds at every threshold. (4.7)
**Non-robust features** — genuinely predictive but imperceptible features; the leading explanation of adversarial examples. (S.11)
**OKS** — Object Keypoint Similarity; pose's IoU, scale-normalised with per-keypoint human-variance constants. (A4.1)
**OPQ** — optimised PQ; a learned rotation before sub-vector splitting. (3.7)
**Otsu's method** — automatic threshold maximising between-class variance; assumes bimodality. (A1.2)
**Part Affinity Fields** — OpenPose's limb vector fields for bottom-up keypoint grouping. (A4.1)
**PatchCore** — anomaly detection by nearest-neighbour distance in a coreset memory bank of normal patch features. (A4.2)
**PnP** — Perspective-n-Point; recover camera pose from known 3-D–2-D correspondences. (5.8)
**PointPillars** — pillar encoding that reduces 3-D detection to a 2-D CNN problem. (A5.1)
**PQ (Panoptic Quality)** — $SQ \times RQ$; segmentation quality × recognition quality. (4.12)
**PQ (Product Quantisation)** — sub-vector codebooks giving $m$ bytes/vector. (3.7)
**Projection head** — the MLP the contrastive loss is applied to; use the representation *before* it. (3.1, 3.6)
**RoI Align** — bilinear sampling without quantisation; +10% relative mask AP over RoI Pooling. (4.3)
**RQ / SQ** — recognition quality (F1 of segment detection) / segmentation quality (mean IoU of matches). (4.12)

## S–Z

**Schur complement** — block elimination marginalising the 3-D points out of the BA normal equations. (5.8)
**Semi-hard negative** — farther than the positive but within the margin; FaceNet's mining choice. (3.5)
**Shuffling BN** — MoCo's fix for the BatchNorm information leak between query and key. (2.4, 3.6)
**SigLIP** — CLIP with a pairwise sigmoid loss; removes the large-batch requirement. (6.5)
**SLIC** — superpixels via k-means in $(L,a,b,x,y)$ with a compactness parameter. (A1.4)
**Sparse convolution (submanifold)** — computes only at occupied voxels, refusing to dilate occupancy. (A5.1)
**Straight-through estimator** — copy the gradient past a non-differentiable op as if it were the identity. (6.15b)
**Structure tensor** — $M$; governs both Harris cornerness and Lucas–Kanade trackability. (1.5, 5.7)
**TIDE** — decomposes AP loss into six error types; run it before changing the model. (4.13)
**Time surface** — per-pixel timestamp representation of an event-camera stream. (A5.4)
**Top-hat** — image minus its opening; the uneven-illumination correction. (A1.2)
**TSDF** — truncated signed distance field; volumetric depth fusion, meshed by marching cubes. (A5.3)
**VLAD** — aggregation of residuals to visual-word centres; NetVLAD is its differentiable form. (3.2)
**VQGAN** — VQ-VAE plus perceptual and adversarial losses; the tokeniser that actually works. (6.15b)
**Watershed** — marker-controlled flooding of a gradient surface; splits touching convex objects. (A1.4)
**$v$-prediction** — the diffusion parameterisation that is well-conditioned at both ends of the noise schedule. (6.13)
